# Asset provenance

The concept artwork in `artwork/brooklet-icon-concept.png` and
`artwork/brooklet-icon-concept-v2.png` was generated for Brooklet with Codex.
It is original project artwork, not copied from a third-party brand or asset
library.

The book-and-brook launcher artwork in `artwork/launcher-icon/` is original
project vector geometry developed with Codex. It shares the open book, brook,
and orange bookmark metaphor of this project's Linux counterpart. No
third-party icon library or downloaded graphic was used.

The foreground, background, and development badge SVGs are the editable
masters. `scripts/generate_launcher_icons.py` derives the phone and Wear main
and debug vector/adaptive resources, phone bitmap compatibility exports,
monochrome SVGs, full-square Play artwork, and labelled design preview. Preview
labels are rendered with the locally installed Noto Sans font; the icon mark
itself contains no font or text. The original studies and production artwork
are included under the repository's GPLv3-or-later licence.

Future contributors must record the origin and licence of every new non-code
asset here before it is merged or released.
